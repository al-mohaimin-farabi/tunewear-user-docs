# What we need from you

For the launch on 13–14 October. Please send everything by **10 October**.
Send passwords privately — never in a group chat.

## Accounts and access

- **Domain** — the website name, and a login to where it is registered
  (or someone who can change its settings with us on a call).
- **Server** — a login to your hosting account, or we buy one together in
  your name (4 CPU, 8 GB RAM).
- **Cloudflare** — a free account in your business name; invite us to it.
- **SSLCommerz** (through BRAC Bank) — once approved: the live **Store
  ID**, the live **Store Password**, and a login to the merchant panel.

## Staff

For each person who will use the admin: **name, email, and access level**.

- **Owner** — everything, including adding and removing staff.
- **Full access** — products, prices, offers, orders, settings.
- **Day-to-day** — orders, customers, products and stock; no prices,
  offers or settings.

Need someone who sees **orders only**? Tell us and we will add that level.

## Products

- **Categories** you sell — for example Bodycon, Cardigan, Hoodie.
- **Who each is for** — Men, Women or Unisex.
- **For each product:** name, price (and sale price, if any), sizes,
  colours, how many you have of each size and colour, a short
  description, fabric and care.
- **A size chart** for each category.

## Business details

- Business name, address, phone, WhatsApp, email.
- Facebook and Instagram links.
- Delivery charge inside and outside Dhaka, the courier you use, and
  delivery time.
- Return and refund policy: how many days, and which items cannot be
  returned.
- We draft the privacy policy and terms; you read and approve them.
